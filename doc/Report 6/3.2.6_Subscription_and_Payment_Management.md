# 3.2.6 Subscription and Payment Management

This module covers subscription plans, payment transactions, and subscription disputes. In the Admin UI, these pages are under **"Thanh Toán & Gói"**.

---

## 3.2.6.1 Management Subscription Plans (Gói Đăng Ký)

Administrators manage available subscription plans here.

### Step 1: Access Subscription Plans

From the Admin sidebar, open **"Thanh Toán & Gói"** and click **"Gói Đăng Ký"**.

The **"Gói Đăng Ký"** page shows the plans list/table and top actions (e.g., **"Hide Inactive"**, **"+ New Plan"**).

Figure xx: Subscription Plans – Overview (Gói Đăng Ký)

### Step 2: Add or Edit a Plan

Click **"+ New Plan"** to create a plan, or use the edit action on an existing plan.

When creating a plan, a modal dialog **"Tạo gói mới"** opens.

Key fields in the modal include **Mã gói**, **Tên gói**, pricing (month/year), tier, sort order, and **Features** (button: **"+ Add Feature"**).

Click **"Create Plan"** to submit, or **"Cancel"** to close.

Figure xx: Subscription Plans – Create/Edit Plan Dialog

## 3.2.6.2 Monitor Payment Transactions (Giao Dịch Thanh Toán)

Administrators review payment records here.

### Step 1: Open Payment Transactions

From the Admin sidebar, open **"Thanh Toán & Gói"** and click **"Giao Dịch Thanh Toán"**.

The page shows a transactions table (user, plan, amount, created date, status).

Figure xx: Payment Transactions – Table View

### Step 2: Search and Filter Records

Use the toolbar (if available) to filter by:

- User identity (email/name)
- Payment status (e.g., completed/failed/pending)
- Date range (if provided)

Figure xx: Payment Transactions – Filters

## 3.2.6.3 Handle Customers Disputes (Khiếu Nại Gói)

Administrators review and resolve subscription complaints here.

### Step 1: Open the Subscription Disputes Page

From the Admin sidebar, open **"Thanh Toán & Gói"** and click **"Khiếu Nại Gói"**.

The page title is **"Khiếu Nại Gói"**.

Figure xx: Subscription Disputes – Page Overview (Khiếu Nại Gói)

### Step 2: Filter Complaints (Subject / Status)

Use **Search** (placeholder: **"Tìm kiếm subject..."**) and **Status** (Open/Resolved/Rejected) to filter.

Figure xx: Subscription Disputes – Toolbar Filters

### Step 3: View Complaint Details

Click a **Subject** value to open the **Complaint Details** dialog.

The dialog shows Subject, User, Description, Status, Created At, and (if available) Admin Response and Resolved At.

Figure xx: Subscription Disputes – Complaint Details Dialog

### Step 4: Resolve or Reject a Complaint

In the right-most action column (header may be blank), click **Resolve** for **Open** complaints.

In the **Resolve Subscription Complaint** dialog, enter **Admin Response**, choose **Resolve** or **Reject**, then click **Submit**.

After success, a toast appears and the table refreshes.

Figure xx: Subscription Disputes – Resolve/Reject Dialog
